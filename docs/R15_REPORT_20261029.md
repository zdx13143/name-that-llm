## Selected experiment and implementation

Hypothesis: correlated punctuation/layout markers may repeat evidence already
present in the style-aware complete ensemble. Only the final evidence strength
changes, from 1.0 to 0.5. Markers, clipping, confidence/length/coverage gates,
all upstream members, ATLAS coverage and Sinkhorn remain fixed. The hypothesis
is untested on real predictions; no coefficient search was performed.

`experiments/r15_typography_replay.py` provides:

- The frozen V32 37-marker hierarchical Beta-Bernoulli calculation and the
  fixed half-strength candidate. Full strength has synthetic elementwise
  parity with the actual frozen upstream source block.
- Five-fold replay fitting typography on outer training rows only; fixed
  identity, text and fold hashes bind the established 2159-row / 120-prompt
  development set. The old 30-prompt holdout remains closed. Its absence from
  local claim files never authorizes reuse.
- A complete dependency inventory, per-fold training-scope/coverage records,
  and a separately bound provenance review. There are 33 dependency roles,
  not 33 trained models; each role must enumerate all underlying assets.
  A manifest cannot itself prove truthful historical training exclusions.
- A bounded no-pickle loader that hashes the same bytes it parses and checks
  NPY shape/dtype before allocating arrays. Actual bundle admission requires
  reviewed producer evidence, not filenames or full-fit checkpoint claims.
- Independent full-strength R14 parity before accuracy scoring. Inputs must
  preserve `_v38_before_hard` at original scale: upstream `--save_lp` divides
  by total weight and changes confidence gating even if argmax stays the same.
- Pooled/fold balanced accuracy, all 18 class recalls, corrected/harmed rows,
  and 2000 paired whole-prompt bootstrap draws. The interval is descriptive
  for fixed predictions on repeatedly used development prompts.

The preregistered future gate requires BA gain >=0.005, positive lower 95%
paired gain bound, improvement in at least 4/5 folds, no fold loss >0.01 and
no class-recall loss >0.05. A failed gate ends this hypothesis without tuning.

R13's `RuntimeTrace`, failure propagation, stage receipts, heartbeat and
submission ID/label checks can support a future full inference producer.
They do not supply OOF or accuracy evidence, so they were not duplicated into
this small CPU replay library. Historical component CV/OOF also cannot fill
the complete-ensemble gap.
