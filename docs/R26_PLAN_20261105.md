# R26 frozen plan — 2026-11-05

**EXPLORATORY / not gate-validated.** R26 combines the complete v15 inference and typography stack with the R24 assignment rule. R24 did not pass its development gate: one fold had no eligible selection and the `claude-fable-5` recall decrease exceeded the preset limit. R25 also failed its selection gate. Neither establishes validation of this full-v15 package. Alpha 1.0 is a judgment: it was selected in two of four R24 folds with feasible selections, while the other two selected 0.5 and 2.0. Beta is fixed at zero. The whole-test post-typography score scale differs from the R24 CV score scale.

After final typography, check the finite N-by-18 logits, exact label order, IDs and groups. Divide each row's logits relative to its maximum by the median strictly positive top-two gap across the whole test set. Apply the unchanged R24 solver with alpha 1.0 to clusters of 12–18 inclusive; other clusters retain argmax. Validate the post-rule parquet before and after its placement, delete the output on failure, and emit aggregated diagnostics only. Typography's own changed-row count remains distinct. The embedded solver and its hash are checked before the same module runs synthetic tests and production inference. FAST_COMMIT runs synthetic tests and compiles `infer.py`; a successful Save is not evidence of hidden inference, runtime, score, or gate validation.

## Input audit and stop conditions

The read-only audit is in `kaggle/r26/input_audit.json`; its completed time is recorded there, but individual query timestamps were not captured. Fourteen datasets have unchanged version and update metadata relative to the previous audit and predate the recorded Save time. This does not prove byte identity. The private generations v9 dataset reports `2026-11-04 09:09:26.857 UTC`, within the minute in which R23 Save was recorded; whether it preceded that Save is **unknown and blocking**. Eight kernels have unknown update/version status; eight referenced model v1 variations remain available but their update times and bytes are unknown; the competition's content version is unknown. Upstream Open Pool metadata is unchanged; the upstream generations search did not resolve the exact source. Historical R23 manifest lists 55 files over 200 MB with sizes but no content hashes; their list is included in the audit. Dynamic glob resolution and the current mounted identities have not been established.

**Do not Save while input identity remains insufficient.** A source confirmed to have drifted after the R23 Save must be replaced by a verified private frozen copy with a runtime SHA256 check; dataset `/version` references do not freeze a mount. Creating copies requires separate authorization. If an old identity cannot be recovered, the source cannot be verified, a test fails, the quota/time is insufficient, or scope must change, stop and disclose. The existing two private pool/generations pins retain their runtime SHA256 checks. No new remote writes are authorized by this implementation task.

## Budget and preregistered interpretation

The chat-approved overall budget is at most **two Saves** and **one submission**; the second Save is only for fixing a failure before submission. If preconditions fail, do not submit. This implementation task performs zero Saves and zero submissions. After a valid Save, verify completion, synthetic and compile logs, pin checks, placeholder schema and ID order, GPU, and elapsed time. A failed hidden submission uses the one submission; no retry or score-driven tuning is authorized. Record exact kernel version, submission ID, status and score when available; pending or running is not completion. Planning estimates are 7–10 GPU minutes for FAST_COMMIT and up to about nine hours for full hidden inference; assignment overhead is unknown.

Against the fixed 0.666 v15/R23 public baseline: a higher score supports further study of this whole package, an equal score establishes only equality at reported precision, and a lower score is a lower observation for this package.
